### 🧩 Applying the Palette to Your Site

Here is how to put these colors into action on specific parts of your NASA clone:

- **Navigation Bar (Top Bar):** Use **Deep Space (`#0B0E17`)** as the background color for the navbar. You can add a very subtle 1px bottom border using **Lunar Gray (`#1A1F2A`)** to give it definition. The logo and nav links should be **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)** , changing to **International Orange (`#FF4D00`)** on hover.

- **Hero Section (Main Banner):** The hero section is where you can create a dramatic space feel. Keep the background **Deep Space (`#0B0E17`)** , but use a large, impactful heading in **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)** . Your main "Call-to-Action" button (e.g., "Watch Launch") should be **International Orange (`#FF4D00`)** with white text.

- **Info Cards & Modules:** For elements like mission timelines, crew profiles, or rocket specs, use **Lunar Gray (`#1A1F2A`)** for the card backgrounds. This separates them from the main page background. Card titles should be white, and body text should be **Muted Star (`#A0AABF`)** .

- **Mission Patch:** The official Artemis II mission patch is also a great source of inspiration, featuring similar shades of orange and blue against a dark background . You could use the patch's specific dark gray as an alternative background for a "Mission Overview" section.

### 💡 Pro-Tip for Atmosphere
To really sell the "NASA in space" look, consider adding a very subtle, dark **radial gradient** or an overlay of a starfield pattern behind your main **Deep Space** background. This will give your site depth without distracting from the content.
